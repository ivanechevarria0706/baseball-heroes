# Phase 8J — batting notes

The surviving gameplay/tutorial and contemporary descriptions agree on a
simple input: wait for a pitch in the strike zone and press SPACE or click at
the correct moment. Therefore this pass deliberately does NOT add a movable
mouse aiming reticle.

The main difficulty improvements are instead:
- later visual recognition;
- no pitch-type HUD spoiler during flight;
- real late break;
- tighter timing against stronger pitchers;
- visual batter loading/tracking;
- identical click and keyboard timing.

This preserves the game's simple control philosophy while making the manual
at-bat substantially less automatic than older remake phases.
