# First gameplay — Lineup / Substitute reconstruction

Reference segment re-inspected:
approximately 12:50–13:50 of
`Facebook - Baseball Heroes - No doy una.mp4`.

## Confirmed visual structure

The original lineup screen uses:
- top resource bar;
- `1ST REGULAR SEASON` heading;
- large blue LINE UP panel at left;
- light gray SUBSTITUTE panel at right;
- numbered 1–9 batting rows;
- small player head portraits;
- small position/grade badges;
- horizontal ability meter;
- AVG at the right side of each row;
- `ALL` and `SPECIAL` filters over substitutes;
- small Special Batter slot boxes at the top of LINE UP;
- team-vs-team comparison block at lower left;
- BATTER SHOP;
- TRADE & ENHANCE;
- HOME;
- AUTO SORT;
- PLAY.

A player row is visibly dragged/repositioned in the footage. Phase 8E therefore
supports both click-to-swap and drag-and-drop from SUBSTITUTE to a LINE UP slot.

## Special Batter Slot popup

The popup in the footage says `SPECIAL BATTER SLOT` and shows grade guidance:

HERO:
- +4 Bonus Combo Points
- Special Ability Probability 100%
- Up to Level 5 Enhancement

ELITE:
- +4 Bonus Combo Points
- Special Ability Probability 80%
- Up to Level 3 Enhancement

RARE:
- +4 Bonus Combo Points
- Special Ability Probability 65%

NORM:
- +4 Bonus Combo Points
- Special Ability Probability 50%

The remake now reproduces this popup structure and exposes the currently
unlocked/equipped slots. BATTER SHOP continues into the persistent Special
Batter Card system already implemented.

## Trade & Enhance

The first gameplay visibly exposes a TRADE & ENHANCE button but does not give
enough footage to recover its exact server-side formulas. Phase 8E therefore
uses the existing persistent Training system for enhancement and a clearly
reconstructed offline trade/release mechanic for non-Hero roster players.

This part is functional reconstruction rather than a claim that the original
Syntasia formulas were recovered.
