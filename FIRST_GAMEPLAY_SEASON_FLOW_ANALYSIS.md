# First gameplay — Season Flow / Level Up

Reference:
`Facebook - Baseball Heroes - No doy una.mp4`

This phase deliberately DOES NOT recreate `SIGN YOUR FRIENDS`, Facebook Share,
or other Facebook-only social mechanics.

## LEVEL UP
Around 6:30 the footage shows:
- `2 LEVEL UP!`;
- newly unlocked items;
- PRESENTS;
- 1 Skill Point;
- 2000 Coins;
- +1 Max Energy.

Phase 8G adds this popup and a separate persistent levelProgress value so old
saves with large cumulative XP do not instantly gain many levels.

## Regular Season roadmap
Around 12:45 the original shows `1ST REGULAR SEASON` with five horizontal
matchup cards. Phase 8G adds the same season-flow idea:
- five scheduled opponents;
- current match highlighted;
- future matches locked;
- city, pitcher and opponent rating preview;
- LINE UP.

## Travel map
Around 16:00, after NEXT MATCH, the original briefly shows a green USA map.
Phase 8G adds:
- home city;
- opponent city;
- curved travel path;
- animated plane;
- LINE UP after arrival.

## Result / leaderboard
The late-game result screen includes:
- YOU WIN / YOU LOSE;
- both teams;
- nine-inning board;
- game statistics;
- rank grade;
- leaderboard;
- HOME;
- NEXT MATCH.

The historical SHARE button is omitted intentionally because it belongs to
the old Facebook integration.
