# Gameplay visual notes — 8N

Primary reference details used in this pass:

- Large chibi batter in the left foreground.
- Distant pitcher centered on the mound.
- Bright cyan/blue sky.
- Cream resort/clubhouse structure behind center field.
- Palm trees and colorful side stands.
- Dark green center-field scoreboard.
- Small fielders visible in the defensive field.
- Compact top resource ribbon.
- Central team scoreboard immediately below the resource ribbon.
- Translucent mini-field display at upper right.
- Light segmented bottom player card with Combo at far left and Leaderboard at
  far right.

These elements are reconstructed with new vector shapes. They are not copied
original game assets.
