# First gameplay — season timeline notes

The first gameplay shows the regular-season schedule again after a completed
match. Earlier match cards remain present and visibly show their result, while
the next game is highlighted as the destination to play.

Phase 8V therefore changes the remake from an "upcoming opponents only" view
to a persistent 30-game road:

`... -> WIN -> WIN -> NEXT MATCH -> future -> future ...`

This phase does not attempt to reproduce any Facebook friend/social widgets.
Those areas remain intentionally excluded from the offline remake.
